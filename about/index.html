About Me

I'm an instructional designer who works on both the learning design and the technology that delivers it. At WeYouth, a youth nonprofit, I chose the learning platform and build and run its courses. Before that I ran a needs analysis for Chartway Credit Union's certification program, automated course setup at NACVA, and helped build a graduate AI and law course in Canvas. I have a psychology research background and an M.Ed. in Instructional Design and Educational Technology from the University of Utah.

Cognitive Science-Based Instructional Design

I start with a needs analysis, structure the course around research on how people learn, and build in the checks that show whether it works from the beginning. My psychology research background means I can read the evidence myself, and my technical side means I can set up the platform a course runs on instead of handing it off.

Andragogy-Centered Adult Learning

Using Knowles' adult learning principles, self-directed learning strategies and the ARCS motivation model to design professional development that keeps adult learners engaged

Cognitive Load Theory Application

Trained in Sweller's cognitive load theory, Mayer's multimedia learning principles and evidence-based instructional design in the University of Utah's IDET program, which focuses on cognitive science

Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

Offering multiple means of representation, engagement, and action and expression, so courses are accessible and inclusive for adult learners with different needs

Systematic ADDIE Methodology

Trained in the instructional design process, including learner analysis, scaffolding and formative evaluation; applied in the Waltz, FiCEP and AI & Law projects
