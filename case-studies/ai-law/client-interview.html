AI & Law — Design Record / Client Interview

Client Interview

The scoping conversation with the faculty sponsor that set what the course had to do.

Coursework artifact from a three-person graduate capstone, Spring 2025. Names and contact details of the faculty sponsor and teammates have been removed.

  1. Review the project timeline with your client

    1. Ask about modules 3 (what are you doing for it the faculty sponsor? Can we get some ideas) Google AI studio is a good use for building a chatbot, don’t overwhelm the learner by being too technical or going too deep into the content

    2. Ask about module 5 & 8 (What legal tools are you highlighting, what does that look like, we aren’t legal professionals so we don’t know)

      1. How in depth should we go with these tools? Use cheap or free AI tools to accomplish research for free, use a broader scope rather than just legal, teach them how to use any tools for research to improve their quality and speed. Examples are plugins for word and other tools that let you connect AI to research

  2. Discuss the strategies in delivering the content

    1. Discussed strategies for teaching specific things, client gave feedback, likes the idea, enjoys seeing our perspective on content he is already teaching and gave insight into learner profile

  3. Talk about the media you will use

    1. Discussed media for the course and ideas for presentations, the client gave us great ideas for creating instructional videos faster and more efficiently.

  4. Describe the learning modules (not necessarily align with the goal and objective flowchart) Highlight modules 2 and 4

    1. Client really liked the modules we had prepared and gave great guidance on media creation. We as designers need to make sure that we don’t ask learners to use a wide variety of AI tools because they would have to make logins for all of them, we need to make sure that we consolidate the AI tools in our instruction so that we don’t overwhelm them with massive amounts of tools or logins.

  5. Did you find something new with regard to client requirements, thoughts and perceptions about the project?

    1. Don’t want them going to a whole bunch of places for assignments, teach strategies instead of platforms. Find one place that has various capabilities or platforms that we could use. We also learned the strategies that the client is using to create the course and make it scalable and easily changeable for future iterations of the course.

  6. What does the client agree and disagree?

    1. Our client liked what we had created and our ideas for building the course, he didn’t disagree with anything specifically, however he did provide feedback about our process and cautioned us to be clear and simple in our build of instructional material.

  7. What suggestions does the client make regarding the project?

    1. Simplify and don’t overwhelm the learner, they don’t know much and you don’t want your learner to have to create a whole bunch of logins or accounts for a bunch of different AI platforms. Keeping our instruction and formatting simple and concise, work on teaching them skills more than teaching them platforms.
