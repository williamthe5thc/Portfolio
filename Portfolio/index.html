Monopoly: DOES MONEY INEQUALITY AFFECT EMPATHY AND COMPASSION?

Money is used for many things. It helps to buy and sell goods and services. Using money can cause the depreciation of empathy, which would decrease pro-social behavior and could lead to problems in society. The purpose of this study is to answer the question: does money inequality affect empathy and compassion? Paul Piff's research has suggested that money does have an effect in changing pro-social behaviors (Piff, 2014). This study is based upon the concept from one of Piff's rigged monopoly game designs that was in the New York Magazine article The Money-Empathy Gap (Miller, 2012).

Ego Depletion in Relation to Test Performance

This research project was presented at the Brigham Young University - Idaho Research and Creative Works Conference. We looked at the effect of ego depletion on academic assessments that were 80 to 90 items in length. We looked at two class tests, examining the probability of a correct answer in the first ten items in comparison to the last ten items. Our hypotheses that ego depletion negatively effected student's ability to take tests was rejected, p=.115.
