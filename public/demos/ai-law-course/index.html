AI & Law

Quinney School of Law - University of Utah

A comprehensive 10-module graduate course preparing Master of Legal Studies students for the intersection of artificial intelligence and legal practice. Developed using evidence-based instructional design principles and adult learning theory.

ADDIE Framework + Universal Design for Learning

Course Development Approach

Target Audience: Master of Legal Studies (MLS) graduate students with diverse legal backgrounds, requiring scaffolded learning from basic AI concepts to advanced legal applications.

Instructional Design Strategy: Applied competency-based design principles with emphasis on practical application and real-world legal scenarios. Collaborated extensively with legal and technical subject matter experts to ensure accuracy and relevance.

Learning Architecture: Progressive complexity design moving from foundational AI concepts through practical applications to advanced ethical and professional considerations, culminating in a comprehensive portfolio demonstrating AI competency in legal contexts.

Module 1: AI Foundations & History

Establishing foundational understanding of AI development and historical patterns

Learning Objectives

  • Identify key developments in AI history and their legal implications
  • Distinguish between different AI models and their applications
  • Analyze historical patterns that inform current AI regulation discussions

Learning Activities

AI Evolution Timeline: Interactive timeline creation mapping AI development to legal milestones
Model Comparison: Hands-on exploration of different AI model types and their legal applications

Assessments

AI Models Quiz: Competency-based assessment of foundational concepts
Historical Patterns Discussion: Peer analysis of AI development trends
Module 3: Chatbots & Legal Applications

Comprehensive analysis of chatbot technology in legal practice contexts

Learning Objectives

  • Evaluate different chatbot platforms for legal practice applications
  • Analyze ethical implications of chatbot use in legal contexts
  • Apply legal reasoning to chatbot implementation decisions
  • Assess legal liability and compliance requirements

Learning Activities

Platform Assessment Matrix: Comparative analysis of legal chatbot platforms
Ethics Case Studies: Real-world scenarios examining ethical decision-making
Legal Implications Analysis: Professional liability and compliance considerations

Major Assignment

Chatbot Implementation Proposal: Comprehensive proposal for implementing chatbot technology in a legal practice setting, including platform selection, ethical considerations, and compliance framework
