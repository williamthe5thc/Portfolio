  1. Are there any changes to the media? If yes, explain

Yes, as we have been building, we have realized that our initial ideas needed to be changed or adjusted throughout the project. We have found simpler or easier ways to present the material and so there have been many instances where we have changed or eliminated certain aspects for redundancy or clarity issues. We’ve also added media where we didn’t have it originally to offer more support to learners.

For Module 5 in Articulate, we decided to add key takeaways after the low-stakes quizzes. Since we provided learners with an AI tools exploration sheet that allows them to explore current free AI tools, it would be helpful for them to review what they have explored or what they have not yet explored.

We also decided to add an instructional video on AI hallucinations. According to the timeline, we originally planned for learners to complete a case study on AI hallucination cases. However, we decided to merge Module 5 and Module 8 because some of the content overlaps.

  1. How do the media support learning objectives

    1. Module 5

      1. Google slides/ docs : Learners will identify AI tools for legal research

The google docs template serves as a visual and interactive resource to compare the capabilities, strengths, use cases, and pricing models of at least three AI legal research platforms. It can help learners visualize the differences between tools and make decisions about which tool may be most effective in a legal setting.

  1. Discussion board

Discussion board allows learners to share their experiences and insights with AI tools, fostering collaboration and peer feedback

  1. Instructional Video

The instructional video not only engages visual and auditory learners but also serves as a critical resource to guide learners in developing their critical thinking skills for identifying AI hallucinations. By demonstrating the process of analyzing AI output and identifying discrepancies, it provides learners with a practical framework they can apply to their own work.

  1. Articulate 360

By interacting with the scenario, learners practice identifying which AI tools would be appropriate in each case.

  1. How do the media serve the content presentation and delivery

Our content in this course is very dense and difficult to understand in a lot of areas so simplifying the learning content down to the basics was vital. Our media decisions had to be carefully selected so that they did not overwhelm or add extraneous cognitive load into our course. We selected media that required learners to interact with learning material as much as possible and also presented our content using real world scenarios and adding in authentic assessment as much as possible.
