VR Interview Simulator for Meta Quest

A virtual reality job-interview practice simulator. The learner sits in a virtual conference room, answers spoken questions from an AI interviewer, and gets feedback on each answer from an AI job coach.

A scripted browser demo. It runs entirely in your browser with no AI and no server, so anyone can try it at no cost; the full version is powered by an AI interviewer and coach through the Anthropic API. Type your answers (or opt in to the microphone). Best on desktop Chrome or Edge; about 20 MB to load.

The problem

Job seekers can read about interviews, but answering out loud under pressure is a different skill, and most people only practice it in the real interview. The goal is a place to practice speaking answers, in a setting that feels like the real thing, with feedback afterwards.

  • A conversation only feels real if the interviewer answers quickly
  • An avatar that talks without moving its mouth, looking at you or reacting feels lifeless
  • The headset has little computing power, and a hosted AI service can be slow or down

What I did

  • A conference-room scene with 11 rotating interviewer avatars (Microsoft Rocketbox) and 7 job types, including nurse, teacher, software engineer and restaurant server
  • Push-to-talk speech, turned into text with faster-whisper
  • An AI interviewer on Claude through the Anthropic API, with a local model (Ollama) that takes over automatically if the API is slow or unavailable
  • Spoken replies with Kokoro text-to-speech and Rhubarb lip-sync, plus interviewer eye gaze, blinks, nods and breathing
  • An AI job coach that scores each answer and gives specific feedback, and an export of the interview to Markdown and JSON
  • The headset is the Unity client; a Python server on a PC does the speech and AI work, and the two talk over a private Tailscale network

What happened

  • Working on a Meta Quest 1, checked in October 2026
  • About 2 seconds from the end of an answer to the interviewer's first word
  • A scripted web demo anyone can try in a browser: pre-recorded interviewer lines with lip-sync, typed or spoken answers, and a rule-based coach, for two job types (software engineer and customer service). Scripting it means it needs no server or AI calls, so it can serve any number of visitors for free; the full version on the headset uses the live AI interviewer and coach
  • In progress: bare-hand tracking with pinch input (works in a test build), a blind comparison of language models rated by people, and a prototype of a second scenario for parent-child conversation practice

About this Project

Interview answers are a spoken skill, and they improve with practice out loud. This simulator puts that practice in a realistic setting: a conference room, an interviewer who listens and replies, and a coach who reviews every answer afterwards. In progress since 2025.
