Articulate Storyline 360 Demonstration - Interactive E-learning Course

A 278-slide interactive course I built in Articulate Storyline 360. Learners make choices in branching scenarios, and the scenarios and feedback draw on behavioral psychology research.

The 278-slide interactive e-learning course, built in Articulate Storyline 360.

Open interactive demo

My role: Academic project. I built the course in Articulate Storyline 360.

  • 278 Slides built in Articulate Storyline 360
  • 26 min Scenario-based branching content

The problem

Dating apps get people matched, but many users stall between matching and actually meeting. I designed this course, as an academic project, to help people date more effectively, from the first message to the first date. I originally built it with a dating app company in mind, which passed on it. The design patterns carry over to soft-skills training at work, such as customer service, teamwork and professional relationships.

  • Building branching scenarios in Storyline 360 where each choice leads somewhere different
  • Applying behavioral psychology research within the limits of an e-learning course
  • Assessing interpersonal skills in a self-paced course
  • Covering the whole topic while keeping a 278-slide course moving
  • Keeping the interactive elements accessible

What I did

  • Branching with 15+ decision points and several paths through the course
  • Scenarios and feedback based on behavioral psychology research
  • Reflection activities and self-evaluation checks
  • A range of interaction types across the course
  • Clear navigation, with accessibility practices applied to the interactions

What happened

  • Built a 278-slide interactive course in Storyline 360
  • 26 minutes of scenario-based content with branching
  • Tested with fellow students
  • Built with Storyline variables, triggers and states

About this Project

I built this as an academic project on interpersonal communication in dating, from first swipe to first date. The subject is personal, but the techniques are the ones workplace soft-skills training uses: branching scenarios where each choice has consequences, feedback on decisions, and reflection activities. The same structure would work for customer service, team communication or leadership training.
