Portfolio

  • FiCEP Needs Analysis

    As part of my internship with Chartway, I conducted a systematic needs analysis for the company's FiCEP program, which prepares employees for a financial counseling certification exam. The analysis showed what was holding learners back, and I recommended changes to the curriculum and to the support around it.

  • Nonprofit LMS Selection & Build (In progress)

    WeYouth had curriculum and no way to deliver it. I ran the market research on learning platforms against their specific constraints, recommended the one they adopted, then implemented their SMEs' curriculum into it as enrollable courses. I run the platform and its technical support.

  • Branching Storyline Course

    A 278-slide interactive course I built in Articulate Storyline 360. Learners make choices in branching scenarios, and the scenarios and feedback draw on behavioral psychology research.

  • AI & Law Graduate Course

    A three-person graduate capstone team designed and built this ten-module AI and Law course in Canvas for Master of Legal Studies students. We ran a client interview with the faculty sponsor, developed personas, ran a cognitive walkthrough on our own design, and validated it with a stakeholder review before handover. The course was not taught while I was in the program.

  • Teaching the Waltz Online Course

    A classmate and I designed a self-paced Canvas course that teaches complete beginners to dance the waltz. I filmed the lessons with a dancer, showing common mistakes next to the correct form, and we tested the course with a small group of four learners.

  • Conflict Resolution Trainer

    A branching-scenario simulator for practicing workplace conflict. Learners choose how to respond, see how the choice plays out, and get feedback on the conflict strategy they used.

  • VR Interview Simulator (In progress)

    A virtual reality job-interview practice simulator. The learner sits in a virtual conference room, answers spoken questions from an AI interviewer, and gets feedback on each answer from an AI job coach.

  • NACVA Course Automation

    A contract role at the National Association of Certified Valuators and Analysts (NACVA). I turned recorded webinars into on-demand continuing-education courses and wrote scripts that automated each course's setup.

  • ABA Reinforcement Timer

    An unfinished Android app I built for a client to time variable-interval reinforcement for Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) during ABA therapy sessions.
